About

Hi, I'm Abby

I'm a graphic designer by trade and a painter by passion, creating contemporary Australian botanical artworks from the Redcliffe Peninsula in Queensland.

Painting has always been my dream, but for now you'll usually find me at the easel on weekends, slowly building each original one layer at a time. Every background is created through multiple layers of colour and glazing, giving each piece its soft, atmospheric depth. I then bring Australia's native flora to life with delicate contour lines, textured details and subtle tonal highlights, creating botanicals that feel both contemporary and timeless.

Each painting is an original, inspired by the quiet beauty of Australia's landscape and designed to bring warmth, softness and a sense of calm into your home.

Every original comes with a signed Certificate of Authenticity.
